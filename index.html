Who We Are

The St. Joseph Secondary School Nyamityobora Debate Club is one of the leading student clubs dedicated to developing communication skills, leadership, confidence, teamwork and critical thinking among students. Our members participate in debates, poetry, public speaking, reading programmes and inter-school competitions while promoting discipline, creativity and academic excellence.

Our Club Activities

The Debate Club provides students with opportunities to develop confidence, communication skills, leadership and creativity through various activities.

Debating

Members participate in internal and inter-school debates that develop critical thinking, teamwork and persuasive communication.

Public Speaking

Students build confidence and leadership by addressing audiences on educational and inspirational topics.

Poetry

Members express creativity and emotions through spoken word and poetry performances.

Reading Culture

Reading novels and literature improves vocabulary, imagination, confidence and communication skills.

Leadership & Conferences

Students gain experience addressing large audiences during assemblies, conferences and competitions.

Competitions

We participate in debate competitions where members showcase their talent and represent the school.
