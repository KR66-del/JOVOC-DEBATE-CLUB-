About Our Debate Club

Who We Are

The St. Joseph Secondary School Nyamityobora Debate Club is a student-led organization focused on building confident speakers, critical thinkers, and responsible leaders through structured debating, poetry, public speaking, and academic discussions.

Our Mission

To empower students with communication skills, leadership abilities, and confidence through debate, public speaking, and intellectual engagement.

Our Vision

To become the leading school debate club in Uganda, producing articulate, confident, and responsible leaders of tomorrow.

Our Objectives

  • ✔ Develop public speaking skills among students
  • ✔ Promote critical thinking and reasoning
  • ✔ Encourage reading culture and research
  • ✔ Build leadership and teamwork skills
  • ✔ Prepare students for competitions and debates

Our School Motto
